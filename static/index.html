The Lizard Lab

Welcome to The Lizard Lab!

We are a behavioural ecology lab, with a particular focus on the signalling strategies, flexible minds and social lives of lizards. We are part of the School of Natural Sciences at Macquarie University in Sydney, Australia. Read more about our research, who's who in the lab, field work, and the amazing facilities we have for studying animals under controlled conditions.

What we study

Three connected research themes

01

Animal communication

We study how visual, chemical and behavioural signals evolve, what information they carry, and how animals respond to them.

Learn more →
02

Cognition

We investigate how lizards learn, solve problems and adjust their behaviour when social and environmental conditions change.

Learn more →
03

Social evolution

We examine the evolution of family living, cooperation, social complexity and parental care in lizards and other vertebrates.

Learn more →

From field to lab

Research grounded in natural behaviour

We study animals in the environments where their behaviour evolved, then bring those observations into carefully designed experiments in the field and laboratory.
