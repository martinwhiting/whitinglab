Facilities / Lizard tubs

Evolution of the lizard tub complex

We bought 40 tubs measuring 3.2 m in diameter, delivered on a large flat-bed trailer and a second truck. After a memorable unloading, the tubs waited several months while the ground was terraced and netted. This gallery follows the process.
