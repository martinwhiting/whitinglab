Lizard enclosures in China

Our friend and colleague Dr Qi Yin of the Chengdu Institute of Biology has built an impressive enclosure complex at Xiaman Conservation Station in the Zoige Wetland Nature Reserve, Sichuan Province. In collaboration with the lab, Qi Yin studies sex allocation and female aggression in the context of mate competition in Phrynocephalus vlangalii.
