Lizard enclosures

We have two different sets of outdoor enclosures located in the Fauna Park at Macquarie University. Six large outdoor enclosures individually measure 16 × 10 m, and a second set of four measure 8 × 5 m. The first lizard yards were finished in August 2010 and the latter set in 2019.
