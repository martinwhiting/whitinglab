Facilities / Lizard shed

Lizard shed

This is our longest-running dedicated lizard room. More recently it has supported cognition studies and housed eastern water skinks, sleepy lizards, gidgee skinks, blue-tongue skinks and bearded dragons.

The lizards are housed on wheeled shelving, allowing us to reconfigure the room, and a network of CCTV cameras records behaviour.
