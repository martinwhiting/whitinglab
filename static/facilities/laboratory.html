Facilities / Offices and laboratory

Offices, laboratory and performance room

We are based in the Fauna Park, a short walk from the main Biology building and adjacent to forest. Our offices are at 205B Culloden Road and the laboratory is at 205A Culloden Road.

Offices and laboratory

The communal office supports postgraduate students and postdoctoral researchers. The laboratory provides extensive bench space, five incubators and a −30°C freezer.

Physiological performance room

A smaller room is used to measure physiological performance. It contains an incubator for warming lizards to their preferred body temperature, a thermal gradient, a racetrack, several treadmills—including a human treadmill modified for lizards—and bite-force meters.
