Facilities / Lizard enclosures

Evolution of the lizard enclosures

The first set of lizard enclosures was finished in August 2010 and the latter set in 2019. This gallery follows the transformation of a weedy, muddy field into our lizard complex. The newest enclosures were set up for bearded dragons, so we moved tons of sand for them.
